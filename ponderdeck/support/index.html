Frequently Asked Questions

What is PonderDeck?

PonderDeck is a conversation card game featuring curated question collections for couples, friends, families, and anyone looking to have more interesting conversations.

Choose a deck, take turns asking questions, and see where the conversation goes.

Is PonderDeck free?

PonderDeck includes a free starter collection with 21 questions.

Additional question decks are available through a one-time in-app purchase.

Do I need a subscription?

No!

PonderDeck does not require a subscription.

Full Access is a one-time purchase. Once purchased, you can enjoy all available question decks without recurring charges.

What does Full Access include?

Full Access unlocks:

  • All currently available question categories.
  • Hundreds of curated questions.
  • All future question collections released through app updates.
  • An experience without advertisements or recurring subscription charges.

We believe you should only have to pay once.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've previously purchased Full Access, you can restore your purchase without paying again.

  1. Open PonderDeck.
  2. Navigate to the Full Access or Settings screen.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.
  4. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  5. Allow the app to verify your purchase with Apple.

If your purchase still does not appear, contact our support team.

I purchased Full Access but my decks are still locked.

First, try restoring your purchase.

If that doesn't work:

  1. Confirm that you're using the same Apple Account used to make the purchase.
  2. Check that your device has an active internet connection.
  3. Close and reopen PonderDeck.
  4. Try Restore Purchases again.

If the problem continues, email our support team with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.

Please do not send your Apple Account password or payment card information.

Are new question decks included?

Yes!

We plan to release additional curated question collections through future app updates.

Customers who purchase Full Access will receive these future collections without an additional charge.

Does PonderDeck record conversations?

No.

PonderDeck does not record audio or video, and it does not collect the answers people give during conversations.

The app is designed to let people have conversations privately, without recording or transmitting them.

Does PonderDeck require an account?

No.

There is no registration, login, username, or account creation required.

Does PonderDeck work offline?

The question collections and primary gameplay functionality are designed to work offline.

An internet connection may be required for purchase verification, restoring purchases, or downloading app updates.

Can I get a refund?

All PonderDeck in-app purchases are processed by Apple.

Refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple.

Visit: https://reportaproblem.apple.com/

Sign in using the Apple Account associated with your purchase and follow Apple's refund request process.

How do I report a problem or suggest a new question category?

We welcome feedback!

Send us an email with your suggestion or issue. We are always interested in hearing what kinds of question collections people would like to see next.
