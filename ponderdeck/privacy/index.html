Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

PonderDeck respects your privacy. We believe a conversation should be between the people having it, not something being recorded, monitored, or collected by an app.

This Privacy Policy explains how PonderDeck handles information when you use our iOS application or visit our website.

Our goal is simple: collect as little information as possible.

1. About PonderDeck

PonderDeck is a conversation card game for iOS featuring curated collections of questions designed to help people connect, have meaningful conversations, and get to know one another.

PonderDeck is published and operated by Robert Childs, referred to throughout this policy as "PonderDeck," "we," "our," or "us."

This policy applies to:

  • The PonderDeck iOS application.
  • The official PonderDeck website.
  • Support communications sent to us.

2. Information We Collect

PonderDeck is designed to operate without collecting personal information through the app.

You do not need to create an account, provide your name, register an email address, or submit personal information to use the app.

The app does not:

  • Require account registration or login.
  • Record conversations or audio.
  • Collect your answers to questions.
  • Access your contacts.
  • Access your photos or camera.
  • Collect your location.
  • Track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • Use advertising networks.
  • Use third-party behavioral advertising or tracking tools.

The app's question content is provided within the application. Gameplay is designed to take place locally on your device.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain app preferences and features may be stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Questions you save as favorites.
  • Your app preferences.
  • Local gameplay or session state.

This information is used only to provide the app's functionality.

It is not transmitted to PonderDeck servers or used to build an advertising or behavioral profile.

PonderDeck does not provide an account-based cloud synchronization service.

Uninstalling the application will generally remove its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups or operating-system data retention.

4. Purchases and Payments

PonderDeck offers optional paid content through a one-time in-app purchase.

The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple's StoreKit payment system.

PonderDeck does not collect or store your:

  • Credit card number.
  • Debit card number.
  • Banking information.
  • Apple Account password.
  • Billing address.

Apple handles payment processing and purchase management under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

The app uses purchase information provided through Apple's StoreKit framework to determine whether the full collection of question decks has been unlocked.

Your purchase is a one-time payment. PonderDeck does not offer recurring subscriptions.

For questions about payment processing, billing, or refunds, please refer to Apple Support.

Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple Purchase Support:
https://support.apple.com/billing

5. Customer Support Communications

If you contact us for assistance, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if included in your message.
  • Your device model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • Your app version.
  • Screenshots or other attachments you choose to provide.
  • Details about the issue you are experiencing.

We use this information solely to:

  • Respond to support requests.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Assist with purchase or restoration issues.
  • Improve the reliability of the application.

We do not sell support communications or use them for targeted advertising.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain relevant support records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Website Information and Cookies

Our website is intended to provide information about PonderDeck, publish our privacy policy, and help users access support.

We do not intentionally use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking cookies, or third-party advertising pixels.

Our website does not require registration or an account.

Our website hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information when you visit the site. This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Operating system.
  • Requested webpage.
  • Date and time of the request.
  • Basic technical and security information.

This information may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues.

We do not use this information to create advertising profiles.

7. Third-Party Services

PonderDeck uses a limited number of third-party services necessary to operate the app and website.

These may include:

Apple App Store and StoreKit

Used to process purchases and verify access to paid content.

Apple handles payment processing and related account information.

Website Hosting Provider (GitHub Pages)

Used to publish and deliver the PonderDeck website.

The hosting provider may process technical connection information as described in this policy.

Email Service Provider (Apple iCloud Mail)

Used to receive and respond to customer support communications.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks.

Third-party providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable contractual obligations.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

PonderDeck does not maintain user accounts or a centralized database of gameplay activity.

Locally stored preferences and favorites remain on your device until they are removed through the app, the app's data is reset where supported, or the application is uninstalled.

Support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary for support, operational, and legal purposes.

Purchase records are managed through Apple's App Store systems.

Because PonderDeck does not maintain centralized gameplay profiles or user accounts, we generally do not have gameplay information associated with individual users to retrieve or delete.

You may contact us at any time with privacy-related questions or requests.

9. Children's Privacy

PonderDeck is a general-audience conversation application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 through the app.

The app does not require users to submit personal information to participate in gameplay.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through a support communication, please contact us.

We will review the request and take appropriate steps to address the information.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of personal information.
  • Requesting information about how personal information is used or shared.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain types of processing, where applicable.

Since PonderDeck does not collect gameplay information or maintain user accounts, the information available for access or deletion may be limited.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we handle.

Because most PonderDeck functionality is performed locally on your device, we do not maintain a centralized database of user accounts, conversations, or gameplay activity.

Support communications are handled through our designated email service.

No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

Material changes to our information practices will be reflected in the updated policy.
