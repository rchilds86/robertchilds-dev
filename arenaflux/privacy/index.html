ARENAFLUX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

ARENAFLUX is a single-player tactical strategy game developed by Robert Childs.

Your privacy matters. ARENAFLUX is designed to provide its gameplay experience without requiring an account or asking players to submit personal information.

Information We Collect

ARENAFLUX does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information to play.

Game progress, including campaign completion and in-game credits, is stored locally on your device.

The current version of ARENAFLUX does not intentionally collect personal information through its gameplay features.

How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored game information is used to maintain your game progress and provide the gameplay experience.

ARENAFLUX does not use personal information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

ARENAFLUX does not sell personal information to third parties.

The current version does not include account-based social features, player messaging, or online multiplayer.

If future versions introduce third-party services or features that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Analytics and Tracking

The current version of ARENAFLUX is not designed to use advertising tracking or third-party analytics.

ARENAFLUX does not request permission to track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Storage and Retention

Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Removing ARENAFLUX from your device may remove locally stored game data. Device backups and operating system behavior may affect whether that information is retained.

Children's Privacy

ARENAFLUX does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been submitted to the developer in connection with the app, please contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ARENAFLUX changes or new features are introduced.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
