Duo Dash Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Duo Dash is a collection of party games for iPhone and iPad, with extra features for iPhone Duo. It is designed to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Duo Dash has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party software that tracks you. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Act It Out, the charades mode, asks for camera access. The camera is used to snap blooper photos of the players acting out each word, and on iPhone Duo it also lets the word appear on the outer screen facing the actors (iPhone Duo only allows that while the camera is running).

  • Photos are kept in the app's memory only for the current game. They are not saved to your photo library, not stored on your device, and not uploaded anywhere.
  • All photos are deleted when you leave Act It Out.
  • A photo only leaves the app if you choose to share it yourself using the share button.
  • You can turn camera access off at any time in the Settings app. The rest of Duo Dash works without it.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device so it remembers them: your sound and haptics settings, which minigames are turned on, your score target, and your best scores in Solo vs CPU.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your Solo vs CPU best scores are also saved in your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data is stored by Apple in your account and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Children

Because Duo Dash collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the change reaches you, and the effective date above will change.
