Duo Dash Support

Help for Duo Dash on iPhone, iPad, and iPhone Duo

Need help or found a bug? Email poles_maned_1e@icloud.com and include your device and iOS version.

Game modes

Party Match

Two players, head to head. Lay the phone flat on the table between you and each player takes one half of the screen. Win minigames to score points. First to the target score wins.

Solo vs CPU

Play every minigame against the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard, and chase your best scores.

Act It Out

Charades for three or more players. On iPhone Duo, the guesser holds the phone open and the word shows on the outer screen for the actors. On other iPhones, the guesser holds the phone to their forehead and tilts it to answer. On iPad, stand the iPad up facing the actors while the guesser turns away.

Common questions

Why does Act It Out need the camera?

It snaps blooper photos of the actors. On iPhone Duo, Apple only allows apps to show content on the outer screen while the camera is running, so the camera is also what puts the word on the outer screen. Photos are never saved or uploaded. They are deleted when you leave Act It Out.

The word isn't showing on my iPhone Duo's outer screen.

Open the phone fully, make sure Duo Dash is full screen (not in Split View), and check that camera access is on in Settings > Duo Dash.

Where is Angle Hunt?

Angle Hunt uses the iPhone Duo hinge, so it only appears on iPhone Duo.

A round stopped when I closed my iPhone Duo.

That's on purpose, so nobody loses a round unfairly. Open the phone and tap Replay Round.

How do I turn off a minigame or the sounds?

Open Settings from the Duo Dash home screen. You can pick the score target, turn individual minigames on or off, and switch sound effects and haptics.
