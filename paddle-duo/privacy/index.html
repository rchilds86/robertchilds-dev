Paddle Duo Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Paddle Duo is a simple paddle-and-ball game for iPhone and iPad. It is designed to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Paddle Duo has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party software that tracks you. The app does not connect to the internet to send or receive any data.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things locally on your device so the game remembers them: your sound and haptics settings, your chosen difficulty and score target, and your win and loss record against the computer player. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Because Paddle Duo collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the change reaches you, and the effective date above will change.
