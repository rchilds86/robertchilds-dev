How I build

Apps I'd want on my own phone

  • 01

    Private by default

    No ads and no tracking. Your data lives on your own devices and in your own iCloud, and every app has a plain-English privacy policy.

  • 02

    Pay once when possible

    Free to try, with a one-time unlock instead of another monthly subscription.

  • 03

    Made for every iPhone

    Built for the whole lineup, with extra touches when a device can do more, like the iPhone Duo.

About

Hi, I'm Robert.

I grew up in South Florida and landed in Durham, NC in 2014. I've been writing code in some form since before I had a degree for it, which I eventually got in Software Design from Durham Tech.

These days I build iOS apps in my spare time, mostly to solve problems my own family runs into. Outside of coding I'm into hockey, bourbon, and cooking.
