Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn on Red Light ("Darkroom") Mode?

Tap the Red Shift / Red Mode pill in the top-right corner of the Log, Patterns, or Report screens. Your preference stays active across tabs so you can log attacks in low-light environments without bright screen glare.

How do I track how long a migraine lasts?
  1. Tap Log Attack on the Log tab when an attack starts.
  2. While an attack is ongoing, an active episode banner appears on the Log screen showing elapsed duration.
  3. Tap End Attack when your migraine resolves, or tap Adjust... to set an exact start or end time.
  4. You can also tap any past episode in the Patterns tab to edit its start/end times, symptoms, triggers, or notes.
Why does HushHead ask for Location permission?

HushHead uses While Using the App location access to fetch live local barometric pressure (hPa) and 6-hour pressure changes through Apple WeatherKit.

If you prefer not to share location, you can decline the permission or manage it anytime under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → HushHead. All manual logging and PDF reporting features continue to work normally.

How do I export a report for my doctor or neurologist?
  1. Open the Report tab.
  2. Choose your timeframe (30 Days, Last 3 Months, or 6 Months).
  3. Tap Generate Clinical PDF, then tap Share PDF with Doctor to print, save to Files, or send via Mail or Messages.
Where is my migraine data stored?

All of your migraine logs, medications, and notes are stored locally on your iPhone. HushHead does not require an account and does not upload your health entries to external servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Medical Notice

HushHead is a personal tracking tool meant to help you organize symptom logs for conversations with your doctor or neurologist. It does not offer medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions or urgent symptoms.
